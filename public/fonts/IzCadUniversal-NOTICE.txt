izCAD Universal Regular is an offline drawing font derived from NanumGothic
Regular (NHN Corporation, 2010) with missing CAD symbols from the bundled
izCAD Symbols font, derived from Noto Sans KR (Adobe/Google).

The derived family has been renamed to respect the reserved Nanum font names.
